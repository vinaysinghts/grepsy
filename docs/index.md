# Grepsy

**Huge logs. Instant filters. Nothing uploaded.**

Grepsy is a log viewer for Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers. Open files with millions of lines, then search, filter, highlight and jump between errors without leaving your browser. Your files never leave your computer.

![Filtering a log with highlights](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/vinaysinghts/loglens/main/store/2-filter-and-highlight.png)

## What it does

- Search as you type, with match-case and regular expressions
- Filter to matching lines with ±2 or ±5 lines of context
- Show or hide Errors, Warnings, Info and Debug; press `N` to jump to the next error
- Saved highlight rules, line bookmarks, and pretty-printed JSON
- Opens `.log`, `.txt`, `.jsonl` and `.gz` files, pasted text, or a URL
- Light, dark or system theme
